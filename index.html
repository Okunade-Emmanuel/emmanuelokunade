About Me

The full journey — branding, web, marketing, results

I'm Emmanuel Okunade, a digital strategist based in Lagos, Nigeria, working with businesses and founders worldwide — across Europe, the UK, the US and beyond. For over 4 years I've helped companies build their entire digital presence from the ground up.

I don't just run ads or post content. I build businesses online. That means starting with a strong brand identity, designing a website that actually converts, then driving real traffic through paid ads, SEO and social media. Every piece connects and every piece serves the same goal: sales.

Across 130+ projects delivered worldwide, I've helped generate over 30,000 orders for client businesses through strategic digital marketing. From Shopify stores to full brand builds to long-term marketing partnerships — the result is always the same: real business growth, not vanity metrics.
